THE PLACE WE IMAGINEMADE FOR GOOD COMPANY

A CONCEPT WITH A HEARTBEAT

Nigerian roots.
A fresh perspective.

Ember & Spice began as a simple idea for this university project: what if the warmth of a Nigerian family gathering met the care of a modern neighbourhood restaurant?

Our imagined home is a welcoming neighbourhood spot, where evenings call for something straight off the grill. The menu draws on familiar favourites: suya dusted with yaji, smoky jollof, fragrant pepper soup and small chops made for sharing.

We’re a fictional restaurant with a very real belief: a memorable meal is about how it makes people feel. These pages bring that idea to life through food, thoughtful design and a warm welcome.
